1. Scope and controller

This Privacy Policy applies to the DataLabs macOS application (the “App”). The developer of DataLabs and data controller, where applicable, is Zain Alsaad (“DataLabs,” “we,” “us,” or “our”). It does not govern third-party software, package repositories, websites, Git hosts, data sources, or services that you choose to access through code, links, commands, or imports. Contact us through the current Support link on the DataLabs App Store product page.

2. Plain-language summary

DataLabs is designed to work locally. We do not operate an account service or DataLabs server that receives your projects. The App does not include developer-operated remote analytics, advertising, cross-app tracking, or automatic project synchronization. We do not sell personal information, share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, or use it to build advertising profiles.

3. Information processed on your Mac

To provide features you request, the App creates or reads information locally. This local processing is not transmission to or collection by the developer.

  • Project content: datasets, notebooks, scripts, queries, reports, charts, dashboards, models, experiments, simulations, citations, assets, and associated metadata.
  • Project identity and provenance: names, author text you enter, tags, file paths, versions, hashes, seeds, source-control information, environment snapshots, and artifact relationships.
  • Preferences and activity: the macOS-derived default author name, editor and runtime settings, recent-project paths, lesson position, quiz attempts, completion timestamps, and other local learning progress.
  • Execution and recovery data: cell outputs, console text, job state, checkpoints, recovery journals, package inventories, and locally generated logs.
  • Device and performance information: macOS/app versions, CPU, memory, GPU/Metal capability, and local workload measurements used by hardware and engineering views.

4. DataLabs collection and use

As of this revision, the developer does not receive the locally processed categories above through the App. We therefore do not maintain a server-side user profile to monetize, advertise against, or combine with third-party data. Local information is used by the App only to provide requested functionality, preserve settings and work, support recovery and reproducibility, display local progress and performance, and create exports you request.

5. User-directed network activity and third parties

The App can enable network-capable actions that you initiate. Examples include installing Python or R packages, using Git through the terminal, opening a web or DOI link, selecting an external interpreter or TeX tool, and running Python, R, DuckDB extensions, shell commands, notebooks, or models that contact a service. Those requests can disclose an IP address and whatever data, credentials, headers, queries, or files your command sends. The recipient’s privacy policy and terms—not this Policy—govern its independent processing. DataLabs does not control or warrant independently chosen third parties.

Important: Review code and destinations before execution. Never assume that a notebook, package, model, plug-in, URL, or command is private merely because it was opened in DataLabs.

6. Sharing, sale, advertising, and processors

We do not intentionally transmit or disclose your project content or local activity information to the developer, data brokers, advertisers, analytics providers, or third-party AI services. We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. The App has no embedded processor designated to receive local App data. Providers used by a support destination may process only the message and attachments you choose to send under their applicable notices and our instructions where they act for us. If a future version introduces developer-controlled collection or another service provider, we will update this Policy and the App Store privacy disclosure before collection and will require providers that process data for us to protect it consistently with this Policy and applicable law. Because the App does not track users across apps or websites or sell/share personal information, Global Privacy Control or browser Do Not Track signals do not change the App’s behavior. Apple independently processes App Store downloads, purchases, and any Apple diagnostics you permit under Apple’s own privacy terms; this Policy does not describe Apple’s processing.

7. Diagnostics and support communications

Help ▸ DataLabs Diagnostics creates a file only at a location you choose. It may contain app/build and operating-system versions, hardware summary, detected Python version and package inventory, current project name and artifact counts, and recent log entries. It is designed not to include dataset contents, but logs can reflect file paths, artifact names, and error details. The App does not upload the file. If you choose to send diagnostics or a support message, we process what you submit to investigate and respond, protect the support channel, comply with law, and establish or defend legal claims when necessary. We retain it only as long as reasonably necessary for those purposes, then delete or de-identify it, subject to lawful holds and the limited backup or security-log schedules of the communication provider. Do not submit information you are not authorized to disclose.

8. Storage, retention, and deletion

Local project files, settings, package overlays, logs, checkpoints, recovery data, dataset versions, lesson scratch files, previews, and caches remain on your Mac until you delete them, the App rotates or replaces bounded state, or macOS manages associated temporary files, unified logs, and backups. Deleting one artifact may leave a reusable image, checkpoint, mounted kernel copy, recovery copy, cache, or operating-system log until its project, cache, or application-support data is removed. Delete a project in DataLabs or Finder; reset learning progress in Settings; uninstall packages through the Environment Manager or remove the local overlay; and use Settings ▸ Privacy ▸ Reveal data folder to locate application-support files. For a full reset, export needed work, quit the App, then remove its application-support data, caches, and preferences through macOS. Uninstalling the App may not delete external projects or application-support data. You must separately manage exports, external project locations, Trash, Time Machine or cloud backups, Git remotes, and data sent to third parties.

9. Security and your responsibilities

DataLabs uses platform protections such as code signing, hardened runtime, the App Sandbox in distributed builds, user-selected file access, bounded reads, atomic writes, and containment and integrity checks for supported managed operations. DataLabs does not add application-level encryption to projects; at-rest protection depends on macOS, your account, storage location, permissions, and protections such as FileVault if you enable them. No software or storage method is guaranteed secure or error-free. Interpreters and terminal commands are isolated for stability, not treated as an additional untrusted-code sandbox; code can access resources permitted to the App by macOS and can use outbound network access. Maintain backups, protect your Mac account, install trusted packages, restrict secrets, review executable content, and apply operating-system and App updates.

10. Legal bases and privacy rights

Where data-protection law treats local App operations or support correspondence as processing by us, the basis is providing functionality or support you request, our legitimate interests in security and service integrity where permitted, compliance with law, or consent when law requires it. We do not use personal information received by the developer for solely automated decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects. Depending on your location and subject to exceptions, you may have rights to know or access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing, receive a portable copy, withdraw consent, and lodge a complaint with a regulator. California residents may also have rights to know, delete, correct, limit certain uses, opt out of sale or sharing, and receive nondiscriminatory treatment. We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined by the CCPA.

11. Exercising rights

Because DataLabs does not receive your local project data, the most direct way to access, correct, export, or delete it is through the App and Finder. For information you deliberately sent in a support request, or for any privacy question or statutory request, use the Support link on the DataLabs App Store page and state “Privacy Request.” We may need proportionate information to verify the requester and protect others. We will respond within the period required by applicable law. We will not discriminate against you for exercising a privacy right.

12. Children

DataLabs is a general scientific and educational tool and is not directed to children under 13 or the higher minimum age required in a jurisdiction. We do not knowingly collect children’s personal information through a developer-operated service. A parent or guardian who believes a child sent personal information in a support communication should contact us through the App Store Support link so it can be reviewed and, when appropriate, deleted.

13. International use

Local App processing stays on the Mac and is not transferred to DataLabs. A network service, repository, Git host, support channel, or other third party you choose may process information in other countries under its own policy. Review the destination and any required transfer safeguards before sending regulated or confidential data.

14. Changes to this Policy

We may revise this Policy when the App, law, or our practices change. The in-app copy will show a new effective or last-updated date. If a change would permit new developer-controlled collection or materially different use of personal information, we will provide notice and obtain consent where required before applying that practice. Keep App Store privacy disclosures and any hosted copy synchronized with this in-app Policy.

15. Contact

Controller/developer: Zain Alsaad, DataLabs. Use the current Support link on the DataLabs App Store product page for privacy questions, requests, or complaints. Include enough context to identify a prior support communication, but do not send passwords, access tokens, full datasets, or unnecessary sensitive information.
